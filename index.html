Kelo Cam Support

Questions, problems or feedback: write to kelocam@icloud.com. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you use, and what happened.

Subscriptions

Kelo Cam Plus and Pro are subscriptions sold and managed by Apple. To change or cancel one, open Me → Manage subscription in the app, or the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

If you already subscribe and the app does not show it, for example on a new iPhone, open the plans in the app and tap Restore Purchases.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Share codes

Every copy of Kelo Cam has its own share code, under Me → Invite friends. A friend who installed Kelo Cam within the last 7 days can type it in Me → Enter a share code, and you each get 5 Plus uses. One use unlocks the Plus features for one photo. Each iPhone can enter one code.

AI features

AI edits are part of Pro and are only for people aged 18 or over. Before the first one, the app asks for your agreement to send the photo for processing. You can withdraw it at any time in Me → AI and privacy.

Privacy

Kelo Cam has no ads, no analytics and no account. Read the privacy policy.
